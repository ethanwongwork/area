# Density as one number (2026-10-14)

Owner: "continue working on the next axis". Radius, Elevation, Motion and Surface each name their setting by
one number taken from a reference component (the medium Button's corner, the Menu's shadow in %, the Menu's
open in ms, the frame's width in px). Type size is a number too. Density was the one geometry setting still
named by words, `compact` / `default` / `comfortable`.

## Decision

Density is the medium Button's height at type 14, in px: **28 (Compact), 32 (Default), 36 (Comfortable)**;
`data-area-density="28"|"32"|"36"`. Nothing renders differently: the ids name the same three levels (−1, 0, 1)
that `emit/base.ts` already derives every height, row, gap and inset from. The Inspector reads
"28 (Compact)", "32 (Default)", "36 (Comfortable)", as Radius reads "8 (Default)".

At other type sizes the medium Button follows the text, as Radius's number is the corner on the default
32px Button: 28 gives 24 at type 12 and 26 at 13; 36 gives 38 at 15 and 40 at 16. The type still bounds the
level: 28 at 15–16 and 36 at 12 resolve to 32 (unchanged, `type-level-min` / `-max`).

## Why three steps, not more

| Source | Density levels | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Gmail, Google Calendar | 3 (Default / Comfortable / Compact) | M/H |
| Discord | 3 (Compact / Default / Spacious) | M/L |
| SAP Fiori | 3 (cozy / compact / condensed) | H |
| Salesforce | 3 (comfy / cozy / compact) | M |
| macOS sidebar | 3 (S / M / L) | M |
| WinUI, antd | 2 | H |
| Angular Material | 6 (0 to −5, 4px a step, each component stopping at its own floor) | H |

(From `ui-size-scale-2026-10-12.md`, "Products" and "Design systems".) Three is the common count; only
Material offers more, and its lower steps stop at per-component floors.

Area's derivation also argues against a fourth step either way, at type 14:

- **24 (level −2):** sm's room reaches 0, so sm is the 20px line floored to 24, the same as md; rows 24.
  Two tiers would collapse onto one height, and at type 12 every tier from sm to md sits on the 24 floor.
- **40 (level +2):** md 40, lg 48. Type 16 at 36 already gives a 40 medium Button, so the step repeats a
  combination the two settings already reach, with 14px text in it.

The spacing step (`ui-dense`) also keeps a measured ratio of space to type for each level (12 at 12,
16 at 14, 20 at 14); a fourth level would need a ratio no product was measured at.

## Changed

- `axes/density.ts`: ids and labels; `DENSITY_LEVELS` keyed by the number. `axes/ui-scale.ts`: the `ui`
  shorthand maps `compact` → type 12 + 28, `default` → type 14 + 32.
- App: `data-area-density="28"` on the app's root (type 13, Compact), the Canvas' hairline contact sheet
  selector, the gallery's fit measurement (32), the Inspector's Select. A Canvas saved with a density name
  restores as its number (`restoreCanvas`).
- Docs: THEMING.md, DESIGN_SYSTEM.md. Specs that say "Compact" keep the word: it is the 28 step's name.
