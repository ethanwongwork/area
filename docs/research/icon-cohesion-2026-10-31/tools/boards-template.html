Should the line weight change?

The same icons beside text at each weight, the way they sit in the app. The boards' "Line at 16px" control shows any weight across the whole set.

The text sets the weight. Geist's stem is 0.085 em in Regular and 0.107 em in Medium (measured). At the app's type 13 that is 1.10px for regular labels and 1.39px for medium ones; at type 15, 1.28 and 1.61.
Area now: 1.125 at 16px, following the size curve to 1.24 at 20 and 1.35 at 24, and following the type in the app (1.045 at type 13). It matches regular text's stem.
Other sets at 16px: Phosphor, Heroicons, Iconoir and Fluent draw 1.0; Cursor 1.25; Lucide and Tabler 1.33.
Recommended: keep 1.125. It is the stem of the regular text beside it. 1.25 or heavier matches medium text instead, and closes the small gaps and counters that detail-first drawings rely on; 1.0 reads fainter than the labels.
