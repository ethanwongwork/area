## System rules

### S1 Field focus is barely visible

High · done · PR 31 (phase 1) · findings B-1

At standard contrast, a focused field's edge is the hover grey (#d4, about 1.5:1 on white) plus a 2px halo of 6% black. In dark mode the rest, hover and focus edges are all the same rung. Buttons, Checkboxes and InputAction in the same form use the 2px focus ring. Notion draws a 2px blue ring on fields, and ChatGPT a 1px blue edge. Verified live: the field focus colour mixes in the focus hue at 0%.

**S1 Field focus visibility** (recommended B; owner picked **B**)

- A: Keep the quiet edge + 6% halo (current, documented in CONTRAST.md)
- B: 1px focus-colour edge + focus-colour halo at low alpha (ChatGPT). Keeps 1px geometry and fixes dark
- C: Make the increased-contrast 2px ring the default for fields (Notion)
- D: Keep A for text fields; Select alone takes the ordinary control ring

Recommended B: both products colour the focus edge, the design system says focus is a system role rather than an accent choice, and it fixes dark mode. D is the minimum.
