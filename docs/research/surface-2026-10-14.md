# Surface as one number (2026-10-14)

Owner: "continue working on the next axis". After Elevation and Motion took the one-number form, the
Elevation session recommended Surface next: it was still named presets (Outlined / Borderless), it was not in
the app's Inspector, and the all-axis audit (same day, HANDOFF "All-axis audit") left two of its problems open.
Review page: https://claude.ai/artifact/L4TCF2W5zcjvBK7xZmaDBN (real components, Surface 1 / 0 before / 0 after,
light and dark, any Elevation). Owner, same day: "go with your recs": the shadow edge and the Select, as below.

## The open problems

- Borderless with Elevation 0 left Cards, Panels and every floating surface with no edge in light (77 surfaces in
  the audit's sweep). Nothing stopped the pair; the axis only advised "pair with an elevation that lifts".
- In dark, floating surfaces at Borderless had no edge at any Elevation, though a lift barely reads on a
  near-black page. Card alone had a dark-only ring (consistency pass O2, 2026-10-12).

## Evidence

From the 2026-10-13 captures (docs/research/elevation-2026-10-13.md, measured in the built-in browser; Cursor
from its app bundle):

| Product | Surface | Edge |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | "+" menu, light | no border; `0 0 1px` rgba(0,0,0,.62), a shadow edge |
| ChatGPT | Menu, dark | inset `0 0 1px` white 20% |
| ChatGPT | Composer | 1px black 10% (dark: white 15%) |
| Notion | Popover, chip, search dialog | 1px ring, rgba(42,28,0,.07) light, #383836 dark |
| Cursor 3.23 | Widgets | 1px widget border |

Every product keeps an edge on every floating surface in both themes. None draws a frame wider than 1px, and the
one "frameless" product surface, ChatGPT's menu, draws its edge as a 1px-blur shadow.

## Decision

- **One number, the frame's width in px:** `1` (Default) or `0` (Borderless). `data-area-surface="1"` / `"0"`
  (was `outlined` / `borderless`). Labels follow the other settings: "1 (Default)", "0 (Borderless)". No other
  width: no product frames wider than 1px, and a 0.5px frame would put every inset that counts the frame
  (border + shell padding + slot inset) off whole pixels at 1×.
- **Borderless keeps an edge, ChatGPT's:** the axis also sets `shadow-edge`, transparent at 1 and at 0
  `0 0 1px rgb(0 0 0 / .62), inset 0 0 1px light-dark(transparent, rgb(255 255 255 / .2))`. Every surface whose
  frame the setting removes lists it first in its box-shadow: Card, Panel, Board card and its actions, Menu,
  Popover, Menu search surface, Select picker, Toast, Calendar and Color picker surfaces, Dialog, Drawer, Command
  palette (inline too), floating Toolbar, and the Language tab's illustration. One rule covers both problems with
  no cross-axis condition, and nothing changes at the default.
- **The Popover arrow** (an SVG a box-shadow cannot follow) takes `shadow-edge-filter`, the same edge as a
  `drop-shadow()` (black 62%, dark white 20%, outside the shape), clipped so the shadow of its 1px shell overlap
  stays out of the shell. Its shoulders join as the 1px frame's do (headless Edge, 3x, light and dark).
- **Card's dark-only ring goes:** the shared edge replaces it, so Card and the floating surfaces draw one edge.
- **Inspector:** a Surface Select between Radius and Elevation (two options, but "0 (Borderless)" does not fit a
  segment beside a label in the 256 Inspector, and it reads as one of the column of numbers).
- Forced colours drop box-shadow; `forced-colors.css` already restores the 1px frame at any Surface scope.

Alternative offered on the review page: keep Borderless edgeless and only restore the frame where nothing else
separates (Elevation 0, dark), as Card's ring did. It needs a unitless Elevation step in `emit/base.ts` and a
threshold no product measures (a 1–2% lift does not separate a white menu from a white page either).

## Not changed

- Inner seams on `border-width` (Panel, Code block, Command palette and Color picker rules, Toolbar and Menu
  separators) still vanish at 0, as before.
- Alert, inline Code and the Segmented / Toggle group tracks keep their fill at 0 and take no edge.

## More contrast (2026-10-15)

Owner: "go with your rec". Under More contrast (`data-area-contrast="more"`, or the OS preference when no scope
sets one), Surface 0's frames come back at 1px. Before, a More-contrast viewer at Borderless had only the soft edge,
which is faint at 1× in dark (above). Precedents: macOS Increase Contrast adds borders to windows and controls;
Area's forced colours already restored these frames. Colours are unchanged; the soft edge stays beside the frame.

- The axis writes `frame-width` (registered, 1px / 0px); `border-width` is derived in `emit/base.ts`,
  `max(frame-width, contrast-more × 1px)`, and re-emitted at every axis and contrast boundary, so the nearest scope
  of each decides (checked: Surface 0 inside a More scope, and More inside a Surface 0 scope, both draw 1px).
  Insets that subtract `border-width` follow, so outer sizes hold (Card 120 → 120).
- forced-colors.css sets `frame-width` instead of `border-width`.
- Cohesion test: `border-width` is only ever its derivation (a mutation setting it directly fails).
- Not changed: Surface 1 (already 1px), the Popover arrow's drop-shadow edge (its frame follows `border-width`).

## Firmer dark edge (2026-10-17)

Owner: "make it firmer" (the open question above). Borderless's dark line is now crisp: `inset 0 0 0 1px` at 20%
white, where it was a 1px blur at 20% white. Same colour (ChatGPT's hairline, #4b4b4b over its #212121 card), so it
still adapts to the ground under it; the blur had spread it into a line barely visible at 1×. Light (the 62% black
outer shadow) and the Popover arrow's drop-shadow are unchanged.
