# Dark capture pass (audit S21c), 2026-09-30

Owner go-ahead to switch the products' themes (2026-09-30). Each product was switched to
Dark for the pass and back to its previous setting afterwards (ChatGPT: System; Notion: Use
system setting; Figma: System theme). Read-only otherwise: menus, tooltips and dialogs that
open without changing data. Captures are redacted (off localhost) and checked for names,
emails, page titles and image URLs before saving.

Captures (19), `docs/research/captures/<family>/<source>--<variant>--<state>--dark.json`:

- menu: ChatGPT profile menu (open, hover) and composer "+" menu; Notion Home "…" menu (open,
  hover); Figma account menu (open, hover).
- tooltip: ChatGPT (shortcut), Notion (shortcut), Figma.
- dialog: ChatGPT Search chats and its overlay; Notion Settings and its overlay.
- card: Notion Home recent card (rest, hover) and Home panel; Figma file card (rest, hover).

Not captured: a toast (none appears without an action), Figma dialogs, a Notion popover beyond
its menus. Notion's card hover registered and changes nothing on the card; Figma's pointer
landed on the card's overlay button, and the card itself showed no edge or shadow change
(noted in that file's meta).
