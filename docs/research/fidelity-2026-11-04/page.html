Fidelity pass

Why Area's UI reads less finished than vector examples in Figma. I measured Area's Workspace screen as the browser draws it: true size (1920 × 1080), a 2× display, Area's defaults, 548 elements, 131 of them painted. Each of my earlier guesses is marked held, partly held or wrong.

Results

GuessResultEvidence
Fractional sizes blur edgesWrong 78 of 131 painted boxes do land between device pixels, and text trimming causes most of them: 39 of the 49 vertical cases go away without it (heights like 16.86 and 35.09). But Chrome snaps box edges when it paints: a card's 1px edge reads as two full device rows (grey 234, 234), not a smear. The cost is at most one device pixel of unevenness in repeated rows.
Text sits off centre in controlsWrong In all 36 controls with a visible box, the cap's centre is within 0.36px of the box's centre.
Shadows are fainter than in kitsHeld Darkest pixel under the open menu: 248 at the default (Menu lift 3), 242 at High (5), 235 with a common Figma kit's large shadow (0 12 16 −4 / 8% + 0 4 6 −2 / 3%). The default menu shadow is about a third as deep as a typical kit's.
Text is smaller and tighter than in kitsHeld (by choice) Area's default UI text is 13/18. Most Figma kits' body text is 14/20. That's 7% smaller with 10% less line height, and nine type styles share one screen. You chose 13 on purpose; it reads as density rather than craft.
Area is shown scaled downHeld The kits and artifacts show components inside a 1920-wide screen fitted into a card. At 380 wide that's a scale of 0.2: 13px text draws at 2.6px and a 1px edge at 0.2px. Figma examples are shown 1:1 or zoomed in.

What would close the gap, in order

  1. Show components the way Figma does. In kits, artifacts and feed cards, show the part at 1:1 (or 2×), cropped, instead of a whole 1920 screen shrunk to a thumbnail. This is the largest visible gain, and no token changes.
  2. Deepen the default floating shadow. Move the default Menu lift toward the kits' depth (the High step gets two-thirds of the way). This is a default change across every family, so it's your decision, made with the foundation-change process.
  3. Keep trimmed text on whole pixels. Round the trim margins to half pixels so rows and cards stay on the grid. A small gain: tidier rows, not sharper edges.
