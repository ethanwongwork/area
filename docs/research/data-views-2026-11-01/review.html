1 · The language

Five parts, each taken from your references and from components Area already has. Every module below is built from them.

Name, bar, value

One 28 row: the name in its column, an 8 strip in the soft field grey with the fill in the series colour, the value in its column. Text never sits on a fill.

Lighter for what isn't in yet

Projected, forecast or still counting: the same colour at 40%, flat. Hatching is the alternative, in the bar at the top.

Eight series colours

Brighter, as the status colours: blue, pink, yellow-green, indigo, orange, mint, purple, peach, in that fixed order and the same in light and dark. Projected is the colour at 40%.

Guides

1px, dashed 2 2, in one guide grey. The label is plain Label text at the line's end. The bars' references, +$5K and +$10K, cross every row.

A key for heat

One block per step, in the plot's own columns from its first cell to its last, with the step edges under it. The cells take five colours, so the key shows five.

Series on the palette

Each series is now a step on a palette family (the top chip, named on the last line); under it, the nearest step the palette had before sky, chartreuse, mint and violet joined it. Then the heat ramps: the series' own (its family's steps) and the blue ladder. The Colours switch at the top repaints every chart with either set.

Every stroke

Four kinds, all 1px. No fill has an outline or an edge; touching shapes are kept apart by a gap in the card's colour.

6 · What changed in round 19

Series on the paletteEvery series colour is now a palette step: blue is sky 350, pink pink 300, yellow-green chartreuse 400, indigo indigo 450, orange orange 350, mint mint 400, purple violet 350, peach red 250. Sky, chartreuse, mint and violet are new palette families, built the palette's own way at the series' hues, so they sit among the others in Foundations › Colors. Lines, areas, numbers and heat ramps are steps of the same family. Series lightness is now Light (sky 350) or Deep (one step down for every colour): lighter greys peach, deeper drops indigo under 3:1 on dark.
Badge iconsA badge's icon (the delta arrows here, check circles, rings) takes its words' colour now, in plain, outline, soft and color badges; the dot keeps the bright mark. Modern keeps its coloured icon, since its words are grey by design.
CornersA mark's corner is now never more than a quarter of its side (it was half), so a narrow column keeps a small corner instead of becoming a pill: the stat tiles' 11-wide columns round by about 3, not 4 on both sides. Wide bars and 24 rows keep 4; 8 strips keep 2.
Series or palette stepsThe Colours switch at the top repaints every chart with the series (today's tokens) or with each series' nearest palette step: blue 300, pink 300, yellow 400 (gold) or lime 250, indigo 450, orange 350, teal 400, purple 400, red 250. Both sets pass the colour-blind checks (palette: neighbours ΔE ≥ 13.2). With palette steps, heat walks the blue ladder. Every heat number reads at 5.1:1 or more either way. The card "Series or palette steps" in section 1 shows each pair side by side. Your pick saves under Choices.
In Area's tokensThe eight colours are Area's series tokens now, and the page draws from them: blue, pink, yellow-green, indigo, orange, mint, purple, peach, each at its own lightness, the same colour in light and dark. Series lightness (Inspector › Emphasis) is blue's lightness, 66 to 78, 70 by default, and the other seven move with it. Charts' status colours are --area-data-success, -warning and -danger. Area's gate checks all of it at every setting, light and dark, standard and wide-gamut screens.
Heat stepsBlue moved from hue 239 to 248, so its dark ramp's middle step no longer carries a number at 4.5:1 with either ink. Dark heat now uses steps 1, 2 and 4 under light text and 6 and 7 under dark text; every number reads at 5.5:1 or more in both themes.
Brighter series, with indigoBlue, pink and purple were capped at chroma 0.13 at lightness 0.79, which is what made them pastel. They now sit where the status colours do, each at its own lightness with more colour: blue #3ba4fc, pink #ee67aa, purple #a964eb, and a new indigo #5a68ee in place of periwinkle. Yellow-green, orange, mint and peach stay as they were. The order (blue, pink, yellow-green, indigo, orange, mint, purple, peach) was searched with the validator so neighbours stay apart for colour-blind readers (ΔE ≥ 12.2) and everyone else (≥ 23.4), on white and on the dark card; indigo lands fourth, so it shows in every four-part chart. The same colours in both themes. Built into Area's series tokens on 2 November: the page reads them.
Stat tiles like the restThe tiles were the only charts drawn as a dark 1px line over a faint wash, and their last point dived because today isn't over. Now each is 30 days of columns in the same anatomy as Requests per day: the series fill, 2 apart, rounded at the value end, today in the lighter "so far" tint. Acceptance is a share, so each column stands in a trough to 100%, as every bar does.
Other's keyThe neutral for Other and Dormant mixed Area's computed muted grey, which some browsers can't resolve, so the key went blank. It is a plain grey now.
