How it's made

  • Every figure is Area's React components or the app's own demos, rendered to markup from this branch. The screens and vignettes are round 12's (the choice-illustrations review). The components are each family's default specimen, the same one the app's Components gallery shows.
  • Bars draws every word as a rounded shape its own width and the height of its ink, in its own colour at 20%, on every label (buttons and badges too); numbers are drawn digit by digit; field values and placeholders get the same shapes. Shapes drops words and icons. Real is the components as they are.
  • One specimen per component, the same one the app's gallery shows first, or a better one (a button with its icon). A few show variants close together: avatars and chips in a row, badges in a column, tags and presence in a tight grid; or a pair of overlapping menus. The drawer fills the right side; the titlebar spans its tile.
  • One scale per kind of frame: screens at 0.31 (M), components at 0.5 (M). Every component and vignette is centred on what it actually paints, both ways, with wide margins (at most 60% of the tile); a small one may grow up to 1.8× and a large one shrinks, so sizes stay close across tiles. Strokes draw at exactly 1px at every size, as in the product. The theme settings' screens are staged exactly as the Screens are. Nothing is redrawn.
  • The skill .claude/skills/area-illustration renders all of it (figures/index.jsx). Use Flag on any tile that needs work; flags save to this page.
