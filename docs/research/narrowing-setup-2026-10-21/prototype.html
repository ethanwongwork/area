How the prototype narrows

Three, then three

A nine-step setting is two short decisions: a direction (Sharp 0–2, Regular 4–8, Round 10–Pill), then the values inside it. The default's direction is marked.

Check what you passed over

After the fine pick you see it beside the directions you rejected and the next step past the range you were shown. Switching there re-centres the check, like an eye test's "one or two".

Earlier picks shape later questions

Every picture is drawn over the picks so far. Type limits density (Compact needs type 14 or smaller, Comfortable 13 or larger). Flat skips shadow softness. With ink actions, the accent is still asked and drawn on links and the focus ring.

The system builds as you go

Picking applies at once; hovering a card previews it on the stage. Each decision lands in the row under the stage, and any of them reopens its question.

Ways out

"They look alike" is a real answer: it keeps the value nearest the default, as an eye test stops when two lenses look equal. Anyone who knows the number can pick it from the exact row.

What's approximate

The stage is a stand-in for Area's components. Corners use Area's formula (setting × height ÷ 32, even, at least 2) and its surface ladder. Shadows and colours only approximate the tokens.
