Area Serif · build

The one page for the typeface. Pick a glyph, compare it over each face, and drag its points. Drag a node to move it with its handles; drag a handle to change a curve, and on a smooth node the opposite handle turns with it. Save a glyph when it looks right: Claude folds every save into the next build, keeping each point you moved exactly where you put it.

    Weight

    One design from Regular to Bold: the Bold is made from the Regular's own points, and every weight between comes from the two. First the four named weights, at a headline size and at 16, 14 and 13 px; then the alternates and the ligatures at each weight; then a slider for any weight, with Georgia Pro and Serrif at their own Bold where they're installed. What is not finished along the axis is listed under What changed.
