Shape and type

Eight directions drawn from the six references: the biennial posters, the blue year, the lilac cut-paper year and the pink contour year. Each sketch is live; Shuffle draws another seed, so you can judge a direction as a series rather than one lucky frame.

What the references share

Type as a grid of unitsDates, cells and pixels are the same size. The text is the texture, and a shape decides where it stops.
Shape cut from contentThe blue year has no outline of its own; its silhouette is wherever the days happen to fall.
Two layers that collideFlat plates and a figure sit on one layer, type on another. Where they cross, the type sits on, inverts or ignores the shape.
One layout, many seedsThe posters keep their words fixed and swap the picture: pixels, dashes, a spray. The tear-off cards are the same idea at small size.
One hue, one inkEach piece is a single flat colour plus black, with labels turned 90° to run up the edge.
