# Source register

All public URLs checked **2026-10-07**. These are descriptive sources, not locally measured captures. Historical labels retain their original context; living vendor docs are identified by access date.

## flat

[Nielsen Norman Group — Flat Design: Its Origins, Its Problems, and Why Flat 2.0 Is Better](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-design/)

Design research; flat and skeuomorphic examples, not a vendor rendering specification.

## paper

[Google Design — Material Eras, Material 1](https://design.google/library/material-design-eras)

First-person account by Material designer Christian Robertson; paper, layers, shadows and motion.

## alpha

[W3C — Compositing and Blending Level 1, simple alpha compositing](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing)

Technical definition and source-over examples.

## glass

[Michał Malewicz — Glassmorphism in 2021](https://hype4.academy/articles/design/glassmorphism-in-2021)

Naming author’s illustrated recipes; an aesthetic umbrella, not a standard.

## acrylic

[Microsoft — Acrylic material](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/style/acrylic)

Vendor material recipe and control examples.

## mica

[Microsoft — Mica material](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/style/mica)

Vendor definition, Mica/Alt and window examples.

## liquid

[Apple — Meet Liquid Glass, WWDC25 session 219](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/)

2025 introduction: Dynamics 1:29, Adaptivity 6:00, Principles 10:31. Descriptive layers, not shader constants.

## neu

[Michał Malewicz — Neumorphism in User Interfaces](https://hype4.academy/articles/design/neumorphism-in-user-interfaces)

Illustrated explanation, Alex Plyuto example and pressed-state discussion.

## clay

[Michał Malewicz — Claymorphism in User Interfaces](https://hype4.academy/articles/design/claymorphism-in-user-interfaces)

Author’s inflated-object recipe and examples.

## aqua

[Apple — Apple Unveils Mac OS X, January 5, 2000](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2000/01/05Apple-Unveils-Mac-OS-X/)

Contemporaneous Aqua announcement: luminous, semi-transparent controls and new Finder/Dock.

## aero

[Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute — Frutiger Aero](https://cari.institute/aesthetics/frutiger-aero)

Curator’s definition, visual archive and Asadal case-study link; not a period vendor style guide.

## windows

[Microsoft — Icons (Windows 7 UX Guide)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/uxguide/vis-icons)

Archived Windows Aero icon vocabulary and examples; not current Windows guidance.

## neon

[Adobe — How to create a neon effect in Photoshop](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/discover/neon-effect.html)

Erica Larson’s magenta triangle tutorial; inner/outer glow and masking.

## gradient3

[W3C — CSS Images Level 3, Gradients](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-images-3/#gradients)

Linear and radial definitions and normative example figures.

## gradient4

[W3C — CSS Images Level 4, Conic Gradients](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-images-4/#conic-gradients)

Angular gradients and color-wheel examples; draft specification.

## mesh

[Adobe Illustrator — Mesh objects overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/illustrator/desktop/paint-and-fill/create-and-edit-meshes/mesh-objects-overview.html)

Mesh points, lines and patches; multicolored ellipse example.

## effects

[Adobe Photoshop — Layer style effects and options overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/ca/photoshop/desktop/create-manage-layers/apply-layer-effects/layer-style-effects-and-options-overview.html)

Effect definitions, gradient strokes and global lighting controls.

## shadow

[W3C — CSS Backgrounds and Borders Level 3, box-shadow](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#box-shadow)

Inner/outer shadow geometry, clipping and examples.

## letterpress

[José Olarte — Simulating the Letterpress: From Live Filters in Fireworks to CSS Code](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2012/07/letterpress-effect-fireworks-css/)

First-person worked letterpress text examples and downloadable demo.

## grain

[Adobe Photoshop — Enhance texture with Grain](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/create-manage-layers/color-adjustment-fill-layers/enhance-texture-with-grain.html)

Grain adjustment layer; amount, size and roughness controls.

## noise

[W3C — Filter Effects Level 1, feTurbulence](https://www.w3.org/TR/filter-effects-1/#feTurbulenceElement)

Procedural turbulence/noise generator and illustrations.

## backdrop

[CSS Working Group — Filter Effects Level 2, backdrop-filter](https://drafts.csswg.org/filter-effects-2/#BackdropFilterProperty)

Work in progress, not an interoperable support guarantee; filtering and clipping model.
