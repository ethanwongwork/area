## Public standards and platform sources

- **W1:** [WCAG 2.2 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [SC 1.4.3 explanation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [SC 1.4.11 explanation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). Success criteria are normative; Understanding documents are informative interpretations.
- **W2:** [G18 text on variable backgrounds](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G18), [F83 image-background failure](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F83), [G209 adjoining colors](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G209.html). Techniques illustrate methods, not mandatory implementation recipes.
- **W3:** [Focus Visible 2.4.7](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html), [Focus Not Obscured 2.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html), [Focus Appearance 2.4.13](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html).
- **W4:** [W3C source-over compositing](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing). Mathematical examples declare their compositing space separately.
- **P1:** [MDN reduced transparency](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-transparency), [contrast](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-contrast), [forced colors](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/forced-colors), [reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-motion). Support descriptions were checked, not executed on all engines.
- **P2:** [Media Queries Level 5 preference definitions](https://www.w3.org/TR/mediaqueries-5/#user-preferences). Draft definitions are not a browser-support guarantee.
- **P3:** [Microsoft acrylic accessibility/fallbacks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/style/acrylic), [Apple Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/). Native adaptation is evidence for fallback design, not proof a CSS imitation inherits it.
- **N1:** Neumorphism.io pinned [defaults L11–23](https://github.com/adamgiebl/neumorphism/blob/8ab93a6bea6493cc464d45a334b5fb7a90d34308/src/Configuration.js#L11-L23), [color generation L89–104](https://github.com/adamgiebl/neumorphism/blob/8ab93a6bea6493cc464d45a334b5fb7a90d34308/src/Configuration.js#L89-L104), [CSS output L160–176](https://github.com/adamgiebl/neumorphism/blob/8ab93a6bea6493cc464d45a334b5fb7a90d34308/src/Configuration.js#L160-L176), also recorded in task 06 R08.
