Materials

Four materials on Area's real components: None (today), Glass, Tactile and Soft. A material adds paint on top of Area; it never changes sizes, text colours, the page and card tones, or the shadows Elevation draws. Use the controls to push each one, then save what you'd keep.

How the materials fit with the other settings

  • Layers stays the source of tone. Glass takes the card's Layers tone and makes it see-through; Tactile and Soft light and shade it. A material never picks its own grey, so every Layers setting still works under it.
  • Glass has a reading floor. Area's text colours are solved for solid grounds. Over the darkest or lightest backdrop, body text keeps 4.5:1 only if the glass stays at least 56% opaque in light and 62% in dark; muted text needs 90% and 73%. The opacity slider can't go below the floor in force.
  • Elevation keeps the lift. Cards, menus and dialogs keep Elevation's shadows. A material adds only the contact shadow under raised controls, which Elevation leaves flat by design, plus relief, rims and sheen.
  • Surface keeps the frame. The decorative stroke (solid, lit top edge, gradient, double rim) is a separate material control, drawn in the shadow list, so the frame width and control outlines stay as they are.
  • Soft is a bundle. Relief needs a tone to model, so Soft also sets Layers to a same-tone gray ground (light 95, dark 24, contrast 0).
