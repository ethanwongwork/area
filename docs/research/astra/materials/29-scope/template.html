Material scope

Which components a material reaches, and how strongly. From four platforms (Apple Liquid Glass, Fluent, Material 3), nine libraries that apply a style across all their components (daisyUI, Untitled UI, shadcn, neumorphism and glass kits) and three historic system styles (Aero, Aqua, iOS 6).

The rules every source agrees on

  1. One hero carries it. The solid, primary control takes the material at full strength. Aqua made every push button "clear except the default button"; Apple tints glass only for "primary actions"; daisyUI and Untitled UI paint solid buttons and strip ghost, link and soft ones.
  2. Thumbs and ticked boxes carry too. Switch and slider thumbs, the selected segment and a ticked checkbox are small raised parts. daisyUI glosses them; Apple turns them to glass while dragged.
  3. Fields are recessed, never glossy. Inputs, tracks and unticked boxes get an inset well (dark toward the light, a catch-light away), at about half strength. No system raises a field.
  4. Selection may take a gradient; rows never do. Aero's list gradient sat only on hot and selected items. Menus, nav, lists and calendars echo the material on the selected row alone.
  5. Pressed flips to recessed; disabled drops it. daisyUI removes the lift and moves the button down; neumorphism turns pressed buttons inset.
  6. Overlays lift, and turn to glass only in glass materials. Menus, popovers, dialogs, sheets and toasts take Elevation's shadow. Frost and transparency reach them only when the material is glass. Tooltips never go glass.
  7. Chrome is where glass belongs. Title bars, toolbars and sidebars are Apple's "functional layer" and Windows' Mica and Aero frame. Their buttons stay ghost, showing their affordance on hover.
  8. Content stays plain. "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer." Cards, tables, lists, text, code, avatars and keycaps take nothing, or only Elevation's lift.
  9. Never material on material. Apple, Acrylic and Mica all forbid stacking. A menu opened from glass chrome is the only glass at that spot.
  10. Keep a residual bezel. When styles went flat (Lion, iOS 7, Windows 8), NN/g measured about 22% more task time with weak signifiers. At its lowest, a material should still let a button read as a button.

Most "echo" families echo on one part only: a field's well, or the selected row. In a typical screen the material is visible on a handful of parts.
