1. The ring takes the part's own colour, darker. The blue button (1), the Aero buttons (2) and the green pill (4) all edge themselves in a darker shade of their own fill, not in black or grey. The levers page's solid ring is black at an alpha. It should derive from the fill instead (a tone ring), with black kept for the board's dark button.
  2. A catch-light is its own lever. Three references (4, 5, 6) put a bright line on the inside edge away from the light, where light passes through a jelly or catches the lip of a bowl. Today it is half of the inner shadow; it needs its own strength.
  3. Wells reverse the gradient. The bowl (6) and the terminal's well (5) are dark at the top and light at the bottom, the opposite of a raised part. As with inner shadow, the gradient should flip on wells automatically.
  4. A radial highlight can sit away from the light. Aero (2) has its sheen at the top but its glow rising from the bottom. The radial gradient needs a position (toward the light, centre or away), not only the light angle.
  5. The transparency floor depends on what a part holds. The glass panel (3) is only about 15% white, which is fine because it holds nothing to read. Its pills hold only icons (3.4:1 as painted). Text needs 0.56 / 0.62 opacity; accent icons need nothing in light and 0.71 in dark; empty chrome needs none. Floors should follow content, not the whole material.
  6. Big soft shadows belong to Elevation, but it needs a bigger, softer resting shadow. The cards (1), glass pills (3) and folder (8) lift with a wide, soft shadow. Elevation draws these today, but its resting role (0 2px 4px at most) is far tighter than reference 1.
  7. Glow belongs on indicators too. The LED (7) glows. Glow should target status dots and indicators, not only solid actions and switches.
  8. Three things are not material levers. Engraved or shadowed glyphs (5, 6) are text and icon effects; D13 keeps them opt-in. Squircle corners (5, 6, 7) are a corner shape, which belongs with Radius (CSS corner-shape, Chromium only so far). Illustrations and icons are content.
