## C01 — Complete role replacement defeats global inheritance

**Side A:** 07 says role override presence is separate from the recipe default; absent override uses the global value at the receiver, and recipe defaults must not block later global input. 14 promises live global controls and “Clear means inherit.” [07-controls/architecture.md:25–27](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/b0768c8d809946b82d6b00139ecc33fe4e57f528/docs/research/astra/materials/07-controls/architecture.md#L25-L27) [14-tooling/builder.md:9–14](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/b0768c8d809946b82d6b00139ecc33fe4e57f528/docs/research/astra/materials/14-tooling/builder.md#L9-L14) [14-tooling/builder.md:30–32](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/b0768c8d809946b82d6b00139ecc33fe4e57f528/docs/research/astra/materials/14-tooling/builder.md#L30-L32)

**Side B:** 13 says `globalControls` is the floating-surface starting profile, then complete role objects replace it. Every leaf is present, even inactive ones. Its generator copies complete resolved controls into both places; there is no override-presence record. [13-presets/contract.md:7–13](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/b0768c8d809946b82d6b00139ecc33fe4e57f528/docs/research/astra/materials/13-presets/contract.md#L7-L13) [13-presets/build.py:27–33](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/b0768c8d809946b82d6b00139ecc33fe4e57f528/docs/research/astra/materials/13-presets/build.py#L27-L33)

**Reproduction:** Edit Glass global `backdrop.blurPx` from 5 to another otherwise permitted trial value without editing its floating-surface row. Literal complete-role replacement restores 5; 07's absent-override contract would use the edited global value. This is a control-flow example, not a recommendation for a new blur radius. Complete inactive leaves are useful reset data but cannot simultaneously mean “explicit user override” and “inherit.”

**Winner: 07's inheritance contract, reinforced by 14's builder.** Retain 13 as a fully expanded *recipe baseline*, separate from user patch presence; explicitly define how global edits affect eligible baseline channels before role patches and guards. Preserve reset/zero distinctions. A different valid option is to abandon global inheritance and make global editing an explicit multi-role operation, but that would revise 07 and 14 and must say which rows it changes. Do not silently treat every baseline scalar as an override.
