## Evidence for the failures

The locked resolver returns light `bg-surface #ffffff`, `fg-muted/fg-placeholder #666666`, `stroke-control #838383`, and `fg-danger #cf033c`. Resolve them through the source above; [inputs.json](inputs.json) stores the exact outputs. Glass’s `transparency=.18` and Acrylic’s `.12` are at the pinned model lines 61/64. Black backing produces `white×.82` and `white×.88`. Applying the pinned WCAG formula gives required-edge ratios **2.485083113777** and **2.882716536087** (see precise generated records for floating-point digits), below 3. The **three** guarded failures are in [failures.json](failures.json), including their backdrops and inside adjacency.

Those decimal values are model calculations, not changes proposed to tokens. Text/body pair indices and role expansion distinguish 29 raw text failures from three remaining required-edge failures. F0 and unbacked-exterior results are separate runs, so neither masks a failed material scene.

## Public specifications

Read on **2026-10-11**, primary sources only:

- [W3C source-over compositing](https://www.w3.org/TR/compositing-1/#simplealphacompositing): foreground alpha combines with the backing; color space is declared separately by this model.
- [CSS Color 4, alpha interpolation](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#interpolation-alpha): colors with alpha are interpolated using premultiplied components. This fixture explicitly requests sRGB; it makes no claim that every CSS gradient default is sRGB.
- [WCAG Understanding 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html): ordinary text contrast and its exceptions.
- [WCAG Understanding 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html): information-bearing graphical/control cues against adjacent colors; decorative borders are not automatically required cues.

The proof sketch, tests and scope restrictions are authored analysis based on these formulas and the pinned task-08 procedure. They are not statements that W3C validated this program.
