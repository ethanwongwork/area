## Public dark and shape guidance

- **Apple:** [HIG Dark Mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode), sections “Dark Mode colors” and “iOS, iPadOS”, checked 2026-10-09 in the rendered public page. Appearance uses semantic colors rather than literal inversion. On iOS/iPadOS, dimmer base backgrounds recede and brighter elevated backgrounds distinguish foreground interfaces. Apple also calls for testing Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency separately and together. **Inference for Area:** brighter local planes can preserve hierarchy over a near-black page. This is not evidence for replacing every shadow with white glow, nor a numeric Apple material recipe.
- **Fluent:** [Fluent 2 Elevation](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/elevation/), “Depth, shadow, and light”, “Shadow system”, and dark low/high ramps, checked 2026-10-09. It separates directional key shadow from diffuse ambient shadow, associates softer/larger shadows with distance, and publishes dark shadow recipes. Windows uses strokes instead of key shadows to outline objects; this is a platform distinction, not a blanket rule to remove dark shadows. **Inference for Area:** use a readable edge and local body separation while retaining the Surface-owned cast when useful. Do not import Fluent's numeric ramp into Area.
- **M3:** [Color roles](https://m3.material.io/styles/color/roles), “Surface”, “Pairing and layering colors”, “Bright and dim surface roles”, checked 2026-10-09 in the rendered public page. Surface containers provide levels of emphasis and nesting; text uses paired on-surface roles. Default surface becomes the dimmest surface in dark theme, while surface-bright remains brightest in either theme. **Inference for Area:** select semantic planes first; lighting is additional modeling, not permission to repaint the page. These roles express hierarchy, not a universal physical height-to-brightness equation.
- **M3 implementation qualification:** [Material Components Android Color theming](https://github.com/material-components/material-components-android/blob/master/docs/theming/Color.md), “Using Surface with Elevation Overlay”, checked 2026-10-09. The current document says tonal surface colors replaced elevation overlays in default components, while retaining an overlay utility for migration. Do not label an automatic white elevation wash as current universal M3 practice. This dated branch URL supports that migration statement only; no numeric implementation values are adopted from it.
- **Fluent shapes:** [Shapes](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/shapes), “When to avoid rounded corners” and “Stroke”, checked 2026-10-09. Joined controls should not acquire awkward corner gaps. Its stroke examples distinguish roles and sizes; they do not establish one scale multiplier for every edge. **Inference:** preserve Area's joined contours and functional stroke policy while scaling optional modeling separately.
- **CSS geometry:** [CSS Backgrounds and Borders 3, §4.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#corner-shaping), [§4.3](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#corner-clipping), [§4.5](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-backgrounds-3/#corner-overlap), checked 2026-10-09. Inner radii subtract corresponding border/padding thickness and clamp at zero; overlapping outer radii are proportionally reduced. Background clips follow their appropriate curves; border images are an exception. Prefer these native contours over independently guessed pseudo-element radii.

None of these sources publishes a cross-style, Area-specific compact-density multiplier. The scaling policy and style translations in this folder are **authored proposals**, not measured vendor settings.

## Area dark and ownership constraints

- [Near-black and floating edges](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#L272-L278): page neutral 975 (documented neutral example `#020202`), raised 850, controls 800; floating edges remain visible over another surface; dark Card has no halo. Preserve the chosen Neutral cast: do not hard-code this hex across warm/cool themes.
- [Shadow ink](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/axes/color.ts#L53-L62): dark shadow is black at 20%, not white. [Surface contract](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/axes/surface.ts#L15-L81): Card dark halo is transparent; floating cast is role-specific; Flat removes casts; Elevated removes decorative width while required edges remain independent.
- [Elevation assignments](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#L156-L165): inflow controls/fields are not made floating merely by selecting a material. [Required edges and contrast](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/docs/CONTRAST.md#L25-L53) continue to govern identification and focus. [Base widths](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/emit/base.ts#L57-L57) are 1px required stroke, 2px focus width and 2px focus offset.
- [Derive and re-emit](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#L20-L27): disjoint axis namespaces, unregistered derived/paired colors. Current [scope re-emission](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/emit/css.ts#L163-L174) is the starting contract, not an implemented material API.

## Density and geometry inputs

- [Row and gap roles](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/axes/density.ts#L60-L69): row 32 → 24, row gap 2 → 0. The older blanket statement that surface insets stay fixed in [DESIGN_SYSTEM](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md#L266-L270) differs from emitted code. This report calculates from code and records the discrepancy, without editing either source.
- [Radius presets](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/axes/radius.ts#L26-L66): Sharp/0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, Pill. [Derived geometry](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/emit/base.ts#L249-L272) rounds controls down to even pixels, floors rounded controls at 2px, holds container radii, and separately limits menu rows/shells.
- [Pill flags](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/tokens/src/axes/pills.ts#L5-L31): independent inherited shape overrides; chip, badge, count, switch and avatar default to pill, while button, field and segmented do not. [Button contour](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/button.css#L66-L82) adds the pill sentinel after the radius calculation. Sharp's descriptive “square everywhere” is therefore not a universal outcome while overrides are active.
- [Switch contour and dimensions](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/switch.css#L3-L15), [sizes](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/switch.css#L42-L47): track heights 16/20/24, inset 2, short thumb heights 12/16/20; compact holds these painted heights. The long thumb changes width, not height.
- [Slider sizes](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/slider.css#L324-L326): thumb 16/16/20, independent of control height; [round thumb](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/slider.css#L220-L243) and [round range rail](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/slider.css#L120-L141) are intrinsically round. - [Segmented nested geometry](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/segmented.css#L32-L68), [Input action geometry](https://github.com/ethanwongwork/area/blob/6f896789a5200bcbd34fe87de458e60ca82141d6/packages/styles/src/components/input.css#L219-L229): independent nested children have an optical floor.
