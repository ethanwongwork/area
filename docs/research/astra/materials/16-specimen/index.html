A study in surfaces.

One component set. Three material recipes. Compare the reading plane, the edge and the space between them.

Make a controlled change.

Changes apply to the selected material in both modes. Clear an override to return to its role recipe.

Role limits still apply. Light is shared. No extra effects on text, semantic fills, ghost bodies or dense rows. Ranges are lab controls, not validated production limits.
