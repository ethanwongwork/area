Manual checks

  1. Tab through links, sliders and buttons. Focus must remain visible outside decoration.
  2. Switch Light / Dark; parent samples follow it while the nested scope remains distinct.
  3. Hover the two enabled buttons: their foreground must remain fixed.
  4. Enable Simplify effects, then revisit the glass page and enable it there too; settings are page-local.
  5. Enable forced-colour emulation: text, outlines and real borders should remain when decoration is removed.
