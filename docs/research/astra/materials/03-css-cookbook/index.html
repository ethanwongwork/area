Astra / Materials 03 / Research only

The layer lab

Inspect the independent ingredients before combining them into a material recipe. Some specimens deliberately exaggerate their effect so the mechanism is visible.

All optical numbers are authored teaching examples, not measured Area presets or native platform replicas.

One ingredient at a time

Linear fill

Two-stop body paint; angle changes the paint, not the entire lighting rig.

Radial fill

A fixed highlight location models a curved body.

Conic fill

Angular colour decoration; not a universal metallic shader.

Mesh-like fill

Three radial layers approximate a mesh; no editable control-point mesh.

Cast shadow

Contact and soft lobes; elevation is separate from fill.

Inner shadow

Inset occlusion suggests a recessed field.

Bevel / emboss

Opposing inset light and shade describe a raised edge.

Soft raised relief

Neumorphic-style paired outer lobes; visibility depends on the ground.

Clay relief

Rounded body plus inset modeling and cast lift.

Inner glow

An emissive inset rim; readable ink remains separate.

Outer glow

Two alpha halos extend outside the body.

Specular highlight

A pointer-transparent pseudo-element above the body.

Grain

A deterministic SVG noise tile clipped on its own layer.

Letterpress

Opposing glyph-local text shadows; the text itself remains crisp.

Glowing text

Soft halos around ordinary readable text.

Squircle enhancement

Uses corner-shape when parsed; otherwise a rounded rectangle.
