What changed since the last version of this page

  • Layer contrast is one number from 0 (Flat) to 8, with 4 the default. It used to be four named steps. Each step moves light sidebars 0.006 darker (cards lift 0.009 on a gray page) and dark cards 0.015 lighter (sidebars half that).
  • Each theme has its own contrast. Light layers and Dark layers are separate settings (since 17 Oct), so you can, for example, keep light flat and give dark more separation. Every ground and every step is kept.
  • It's in the app. The Inspector has Light ground, Dark ground, Light layers and Dark layers under Color. The defaults, 100 + 4 in light and 8 + 4 in dark, are what the app uses now.
