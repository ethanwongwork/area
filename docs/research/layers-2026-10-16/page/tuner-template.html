Layers: the page and what sits on it

Two choices per theme: the ground (the page's lightness, in 1% steps from white) and the layer contrast. Every combination is checked below for readable text, visible controls and visible edges. Each scene is Area's own CSS.

How it works

  • Ground is the page's OKLCH lightness: light 100 (white) down to 90 in 1% steps; dark 0 (black), 8 (Area's near-black today), then 12 to 24 in 2% steps (below 12 a 1% step doesn't show on a screen).
  • Layer contrast (Flat, Low, Default, High) sets how far cards, menus and sidebars sit from the page. Direction follows the ground: on white, cards stay white and sidebars go darker; on a gray page, cards lift toward white; in dark, layers lift lighter, starting from the first visible dark when the page is darker than that.
  • Controls always show. A field, a row's hover and a selected row step from the ground they sit on, never less than a visible step, at Flat too. In dark they never sit under L 0.20, so on Black a selected row is a clear #282828-ish plate (it was black on black).
  • Text and edges hold their contrast. Muted text and control edges keep today's colour wherever it passes, and darken (light) or lighten (dark) just enough to keep 4.5:1 and 3:1 on the deepest surface they sit on. Card, menu and sidebar frames follow the ground too, so a frame never disappears into a gray page.
  • Defaults. Light 100 + Default is today's page, cards, sidebar, fields, edges and text exactly; sidebar row plates now step from the sidebar's own tone, one step firmer than today (#efefef / #e6e6e6 for #f3f3f3 / #eeeeee). Dark 8 + Default keeps today's #020202 page and brings cards and sidebars closer (#121212, #0b0b0b; today #1d1d1d, #282828).
  • The ramp stays for text, borders, fills and hues; only the layer tones are computed. Exact numbers stay available under Custom, as tokens a product team could set.
