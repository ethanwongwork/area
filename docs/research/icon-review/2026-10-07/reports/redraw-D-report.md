## gamepad
- **Verdict:** round 1 "replace → Lucide". Round 2 (on the Lucide-based redraw) "replace → Lucide: handles need to be bigger". Group 8's rebuild today (bridge at 11, grips 3 wide) still read faint at 16.
- **Reference:** Lucide `gamepad-2.svg`. Its grips read as handles; `gamepad.svg` is a plain rectangle with no grips. Also looked at: Fluent `games_16_regular/filled` (a stadium) and Phosphor `game-controller` (long splayed handles).
- **Reference construction (24 grid):** a 20 × 14 body with r 4 top corners and slightly flared sides. The bottom has two round grip lobes: an outer r 3 corner and a soft inner curve into a 45° step. The bridge is raised 3 and joined to the steps by r 2 fillets, and its flat is about 21% of the width. The + d-pad is at a third of the width. Two buttons sit on a 45° diagonal, 1 above and 1 below the d-pad's centre line. Scaled to Area's 13-wide keyline, the grip depth is about 1.95.
- **New construction:** a 13 × 10 body (x 1.5–14.5, y 3–13; the full horizontal keyline) with r 3 top corners. The grips are 2.5 deep: the bridge is at y 10.5, flat from 6.5 to 9.5, and reached by 45° steps (4,13)→(6.5,10.5) and its mirror (on the lattice, x ± y whole). Corner radii: r 1.5 fillets at the bridge, and r 1.5 inside the foot. The outer foot corner is r 1.879, so the two foot arcs meet tangentially and each grip ends in one round lobe; r 2 left a 0.12 backtrack at the foot. In source terms: `icon-geometry fillet "1.5,3 14.5,3 14.5,13 12,13 9.5,10.5 6.5,10.5 4,13 1.5,13" "3,3,1.879,1.5,1.5,1.5,1.5,1.879"`, with the zero-length L segments removed. The 3px + d-pad is at 5.5, 7. The buttons are ⌀1.5 at 10, 8 and 12, 6: Lucide's 45° pair straddling the d-pad's line. Filled: the body is solid, the d-pad a knockout, the buttons holes (unchanged roles).
- **How the note is answered:** the grips are deeper (2.5, against Lucide's proportional 1.95 and the previous 2 and 1.5). They are wider at the top: each runs from the edge to the bridge step, 5 wide at the bridge line. The body uses the full keyline width, and the feet are round lobes instead of square-ish corners, so the handles read as handles at 16 in both styles. Deeper 45° notches at depth 3 (candidates g1, g5, g7 and g11) made a V that read as a frowning mask, so the flat Lucide bridge was kept.
- **Verification:** `--only` lint ok. Ink 14.12 × 11.12, centred 8, 8 (was 8, 8.25). Gaps 1.23, 1.34, 1.19 and 1.33 tight, 1.85 standard, 3.79 open; the previous detail gap of 1.0 between the buttons is now tight. Slivers: none at 16 and none at 48. Looked at 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96, outline and filled, beside Lucide, Fluent and Phosphor.

## bot-generator
- **Verdict:** round 1 "rounded stroke arrow" (the curled tip of 10-03). Round 2 "can you make the wizards hat look more like a wizards hat and make it bigger".
- **Reference:** none of the libraries has a wizard hat (Lucide, Phosphor, Hugeicons and Fluent searched). The object's telling details are a tall cone whose tip bends over and droops to one side, on a wide brim. Group 8's c2 (crooked tip) was the starting point. Its curl read as a wave or soft-serve when filled out (w2, w4–w6), so the bend was made a straight-edged fold.
- **Family relation:** kept from bot-agent and bot-builder: the head opened at 2.893 / 13.107, the brim `M1.5 6.25Q8 8 14.5 6.25`, eyes `M5 9V10.5M11 9V10.5` and the ⌀1.5 mouth at 8, 11.25. The hat's feet sit on the brim at bot-agent's crown feet (3.5 and 12.5), closed along the brim (`Q8 7.544`).
- **New construction:** a cone from the left foot (3.5, 6.706). The left side `C5.5 5 7.75 1.5 9.25 1.5` rises to a smooth apex on the 1.5 live-area line (tangent horizontal, no kink). The top edge `C10.75 1.5 12.25 2 13 3` runs over to a drooping tip at 13, 3. A straight underside `L11 3.5` folds back to the knee. The right side `C11.25 4.5 11.75 5.75 12.5 6.706` runs down to the right foot. The tip is a natural point (67°, over the 60° floor). The fold at 11, 3.5 is the crease where the tip bends. The 10-03 band (`line` + `gap`) is dropped: no band position fits between the cone and the brim. At y 5 it left 0.79–0.85 against the brim, and the old band at 4.25 is what forced `data-counter="sliver"`, so that attribute is removed too. Filled: the whole cone is solid (the hat's main mass, per "Audit, 2026-10-05"). The brim and head stay lines, as in bot-agent and bot-builder.
- **How the note is answered:** the hat now reads as a wizard's hat, a cone with the tip bent over and down to the right on the wide brim. It is bigger in every way the family allows: the same height (1.5 to the brim, like every hat) but much more mass. The old cone had concave sides (a pagoda point); the new one has a full base from 3.5 to 12.5 and reaches 13 with its tip. Filled, it is one solid hat instead of a small tip over a band. Narrower leaning cones (w7–w9) read more pointed but smaller, and feet inside 3.5 tripped a near miss with the head's open ends.
- **Verification:** `--only` lint ok. Ink 14.12 × 13.12, centre 8, 7.5 (the same as the other brimmed bots). Gaps are the family's: 1.88, 0.94, 0.92, 2.81, 0.92, 2.81, 1.78. Counters 3.9 and 3.18. Slivers: none at 16 and none at 48. Looked at 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96, outline and filled, beside all seven hatted bots.

## translate
- **Verdict:** round 1 "replace → Phosphor: the kanji doesn't look correct" (applied 10-03 as Phosphor's 文A). Round 2 "replace → Fluent".
- **Reference:** Fluent `translate_16_regular/filled`, also `_20_` and `_24_`. Fluent's translate is not 文A: it is a Latin "a" top left beside a hiragana "あ" bottom right. Lucide's languages and Phosphor's translate (both 文A) were looked at only as evidence.
- **Reference construction:** the "a" is two-storey. A top arch starts with a hook at the upper left and runs into a straight stem on the right (stem to the baseline). A bowl hangs from the stem by a flat top edge, rounds at the left and returns up into the stem above its foot. Its box is about 2.5–6.5 × 3.2–8.5 on the centre-line, and the bowl is about 1.5 : 1. The "あ" (7.3–14.7 × 5.5–14 ink) has three strokes. (1) A short bar across the top. (2) A long stroke crossing the bar near its middle, running down and slightly left. (3) A stroke that starts right of stroke 2 under the bar, runs down-left across it, loops at the lower left, rises right across stroke 2 again and sweeps a large clockwise arc down the right side, ending low and turning left. That is the correct stroke structure of あ, so the character is right.
- **New construction:**
  - a, two-storey: the arch is `M3 3C3.5 2.67 4.1 2.5 4.75 2.5C5.85 2.5 6.5 3.15 6.5 4.25V8.5`, a smooth arch (top 2.5) into an x.5 stem at 6.5 ending on 8.5. The bowl is `M6.5 5.5H3.5A1.5 1.5 0 0 0 3.5 8.5C4.75 8.5 5.85 8.15 6.5 7.5`: a flat top at 5.5, an r 1.5 round left side reaching 2, and a return into the stem 1 above its foot, as Fluent's. Its counter is 1.93 (standard).
  - あ: bar `M9 6H13.5`. Stroke 2 is `M11 4.5C11 7.5 10.5 9.75 10.5 12.25`, which crosses the bar at 11 and ends inside stroke 3 where they cross, so no cap pokes out. Stroke 3 is one smooth path `M12.75 8.5C12 10.5 10.5 12.5 9.25 13.25C8.5 13.7 7.5 13.5 7.5 12.5C7.5 10.75 10 9.5 12 9.5C13.25 9.5 14 10.4 14 11.5C14 12.9 12.9 13.75 11.75 13.75`: diagonal, loop at the lower left (counter 1.47, tight), rise across stroke 2, then the big right arc to 14 and the end turning left.
  - Strokes of one glyph cross flat (same plane). Line glyph: it fills by weight, as Fluent's filled is the same drawing heavier. `data-keyline="square"`, `data-angles="free"`.
- **How the note is answered:** the icon is now Fluent's design (a + あ) on Area's line, in Fluent's arrangement (a high left, あ low right, overlapping in height). The あ has the correct three strokes in the correct order of shapes.
- **Verification:** `--only` lint ok. Ink 13.12 × 12.37, centred 8, 8.13. Gaps 1.38, 1.42 and 1.38 tight, 1.84 standard, the rest open (the bar is a tight 2.5 from the a's stem). Counters 1.93 and 1.47. Slivers: none at 16 and none at 48. Looked at 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96, outline and filled, beside Fluent 16 and 24 regular and filled. Candidate history is in `redraw-D/tr/` (t1 had a 0.69 loop counter and a bar-to-stroke near miss; t3 sat 0.25 right of centre until the bowl reached 2).
