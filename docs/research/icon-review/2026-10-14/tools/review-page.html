Long strokes on whole pixels

295 icons

295 icons draw a long straight stroke (3 units or more) on a whole-pixel line: folders, clipboards, laptop, printer, wallet, book and others. On 2× screens they are sharp. On 1× screens, such as many external monitors, the stroke smears across two grey rows, as qr-code did. The Oct 10 pass fixed this for a few icons (wifi, git-commit, blur, caps-lock) by moving the stroke half a pixel, even off centre.

A blanket half-pixel shift keeps every 45° line on the lattice, but it is not the fix. Parts already on half pixels go soft; see clipboard's clip below. Each icon needs its frame moved and its parts re-placed, then a look at 1× and 2×.
