## How the six built so far sit against it

The board is brighter, more playful and more hand-made than 001–006, which lean Swiss and
sober. 001 Stretch and 006 Poster fit theme 8; 004 Quarter fits 6; 003 Mesh touches 3's
gradients. Nothing yet covers the board's two biggest themes, type along a path (1) and
grids of dots and stitches (2).
